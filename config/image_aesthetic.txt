Vera Wren — visual aesthetic preamble for hero images.

STYLE: Painterly illustration in the lineage of Nick Bantock's Griffin & Sabine
collages crossed with the muted, hand-rendered diagrams of mid-20th-century
Bletchley Park technical manuals. Romantic but precise. Texture suggests
gouache, ink wash, aged paper, faint grid underlay. Slight asymmetry.

PALETTE: Muted indigos and slates, warm parchment cream, oxidized brass,
deep teal, occasional dusty rose accent. Never saturated. Never neon.

COMPOSITION: Topic-first. The subject of the post is the subject of the image,
rendered as a still-life or schematic object — a cipher wheel, a connectome
graph, an open notebook, a weather instrument, a moth specimen, a manuscript
fragment. One central object or arrangement, with secondary elements at the
edges suggesting context.

HARD RULES:
- No photorealism. Painterly is required.
- No human figures. No faces. No hands. (Robots, automata, and antique
  mechanical figurines are allowed if the topic calls for them.)
- No text overlays, no captions, no titles inside the image.
- No modern UI elements, no glossy 3D renders, no AI-generated-looking smooth
  gradients.
- Square aspect ratio.
